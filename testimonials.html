Ten testimonials

  • I used a drugstore bar for years and never thought about it. Two weeks with this and my skin stopped feeling tight the moment I got out of the shower. I don't think I can go back.
  • The lather is the whole thing. Thick, low, and it stays put instead of running straight off you. I use a fraction of what I used to and the bar has lasted nearly three months.
  • We have hard water and most soap leaves a film I can feel for an hour afterwards. This one rinses clean. My skin feels soft rather than squeaky.
  • The scent is not what I expected from oak and moss. Damp and green and slightly sweet, like the floor of a forest after rain. My whole bathroom smells like a hike.
  • I am particular about what goes on my face. Eight ingredients, every one of which I recognize, and it has not left my skin dry once.
  • My hands take a beating at work and by February they are usually rough as sandpaper. This is the first winter they have stayed soft the whole way through.
  • I shave with it. The clay gives just enough slip that I stopped buying shaving cream — one less bottle in the shower and one less thing in the trash.
  • Most scented soap is too much for me and I end up back with unscented. This one is present without being loud. It is the first scented bar I have ever finished.
  • I bought it for my husband because the packaging was beautiful. Now I am the one using it and he is quietly furious about it.
  • It arrived wrapped in paper with no plastic anywhere and the whole carton went into the compost. A small thing, but it is the reason I keep going back.
